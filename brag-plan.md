# Brag Plan: Mobbin

## What is this app?
Mobbin is the world's most comprehensive UI & UX design reference library, housing 400,000+ searchable real-world mobile and web app screens, flows, and design patterns.

## The angle
The design library that world-class product teams and autonomous AI agents rely on to skip hundreds of hours of design guesswork. High-contrast, sleek motion graphics (inspired by Muse and modern linear design aesthetics) where floating UI frames, searchable filters, and agent connectors animate with snappy physics.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The headline drops on a dark canvas: "Where teams and agents find designs that work", as high-res mobile app screens cascade into a 3D isometric stack.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **400,000+ Searchable Screens**: Rapid cycling through iOS, Android, and Web patterns.
- **Real User Flows**: Complete onboarding, checkout, and paywall teardowns revealed side-by-side.
- **The AI / MCP Era**: "Give your AI agents access to Mobbin" showing the Model Context Protocol connection.

## Outro / punchline
"Save hours of UI & UX research. Find designs that work at mobbin.com"

## User flow worth showing
Search for design pattern (e.g. "Paywall" or "Onboarding") → filter by platform (iOS, Android, Web) → deep-dive into full multi-step flow → export to Figma or agent.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: sleek, high-contrast, modern UI motion graphics like Muse
- Interpretation: Crisp typography, dark obsidian & white contrast, snappy cubic eases, floating device frames, and precise rhythmic cuts.
